About SalaryWise India

Our purpose

SalaryWise India is a free educational toolkit designed to make salary, tax, borrowing and investing calculations easier to understand.

How estimates work

Each calculator uses clearly stated assumptions and formulas. Results are estimates, not personalised financial, tax or legal advice.

Built for India

Amounts are displayed in Indian rupees and the tools focus on common Indian salary, tax, PF, gratuity, loan and investment questions.

Disclaimer: SalaryWise India provides educational estimates only. Tax rules, PF rules, bank policies, interest rates and employment terms can change. Always verify important decisions with the relevant government authority, employer, lender or qualified professional.
